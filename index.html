About

The Yale Men's Club Water Polo Team is the highest level of men's water polo on campus: a student-run program with a close-knit community competing in 7+ tournaments a year in the New England Division of the Collegiate Water Polo Association.

All experience levels are welcome. Whether you're a seasoned player or picking up a ball for the first time, we look forward to seeing you at practice.
